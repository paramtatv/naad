For listening

Nothing lost. Nothing hidden. Nothing imagined back.

A lossy file keeps what a psychoacoustic model guessed you would not notice. A lossless one keeps everything, so there is nothing for the decoder to guess at. Two correct lossless decoders return the same bytes, and Naad is one of them. What makes it different is what it refuses to do: it never rounds, never approximates, never drifts.

The recording

The decay on the cymbal. The room behind the strings.

The breath before the note, the chair creak at the back of the hall, the tail of the reverb as it falls under the noise floor. All of it was in the master. All of it is in the file.

The playback

The same integers, every time, on every machine.

Naad decodes with whole numbers only. There is no floating-point step anywhere that could round one way on your phone and another way on your DAC. Play it a thousand times and the samples never change.

The years

Still yours when the service is gone.

The decoder is 1,182 lines you can read and keep. No subscription, no runtime, no vendor to outlive. The album you decode today is the album you decode when your children find the drive.

What ships today, and what does not
  • GreenFrame decoder: header with all four code spaces, all four subframe bodies, partitioned Rice with escapes, every stereo pairing, both CRCs. One or two channels.
  • GreenSassembly: the compiler compiled itself byte for byte on 2026-09-22 and has on every change since.
  • Not builtThe stream: walking a file of frames, seeking, metadata, cover art, recovery after a damaged frame. This is what stands between the decoder and a player.
  • Not builtMore than two channels: surround parses its header and is refused rather than guessed at.
  • Not builtAttestation: one digest on three instruction sets. The machinery exists in Darśana; it has not been run on Naad.

Sassembly, in one paragraph

Naad is written in Sassembly, a systems language whose every keyword is Sanskrit, written in itself, compiling to 64-bit RISC-V. The whole toolchain is 12 MB: compiler, assembler, linker and interpreter, with no LLVM, no GCC and nothing fetched at build time. Feed its source to itself and it produces a compiler image; feed the source to that image and it produces another, byte for byte identical. The decoder is called निःशेषम्, niḥśeṣam, "with no remainder", which is not a poetic name for lossless decoding but a definition of it. नाद, nāda, is sound considered as vibration rather than as signal.

Where we are still behind

The problems we have not solved yet.

A codec that will not tell you where it is weak is one you cannot plan around. These are the places Naad is behind the state of the art, what the gap measures, and what we already know about closing it.

Open · throughput

Raw decode speed

Mature C decoders run well ahead of six times real time. Naad is fast enough to play anything on anything, and not yet fast enough to be the obvious choice for a battery-powered device decoding for ten hours.

Profiled to the instruction. CRC-16 alone is 37.1% of decode time: it re-traverses every bit of the frame. An octet-wise path is worth 1.60× on the whole decoder before any other work.
Open · compression

The encoder is Python, and its stream is not yet emitted

Levinson-Durbin, apodization and the partition-order search are real and measured at parity with flac -8. None of it is native yet, and its size figure is an exact bit count rather than a file.

The join, that a stream of exactly that size decodes back, is not closed. Until it is, the encoder figure and the decoder figure are two claims, and the page keeps them apart.
Not built · format

Container, seeking, metadata

The frame decoder is complete. Walking a file of frames, seeking to a timestamp, reading tags and cover art, and recovering from a damaged frame are the layer above it, and nothing implements that layer yet.

Frame layer complete. Stream layer not built. Until it is, Naad is a verified codec core and not a player.
Not built · channels

Beyond two channels

Mono and every stereo pairing are done and verified. Surround and ambisonic material parses its header today and is then refused rather than guessed at.

An unimplemented path that answers is not the same as one that quietly returns something plausible.
Research · perceptual

Lossy, without the usual bargain

The best-sounding codecs in the world are now neural, and they run in floating point, so the same stream decodes to different files on different machines. A decoder that is exact by construction could change that. The papers exist; nothing ships it.

Nothing here ships yet. We will not describe it as available until it is measured.
Research · the long horizon

Sound as structure, not as samples

A recording is a list of amplitudes. Music is not. The Indian tradition spent centuries building a vocabulary for pitch, interval and ornament that is structural rather than statistical, and a codec that understood structure would not compete on the same axis at all.

This is the thesis the programme is chasing. It is research, and this page marks it as research.
